Meet Our Founder

SA

Saheed Adesanya

Founder & CEO

Logistics Expert Entrepreneur
"
We built Fastclear to give Nigerian businesses the same logistics advantage that global brands enjoy.

Saheed Adesanya founded Fastclear Cargo in 2018 with a singular vision: to eliminate the frustration and unpredictability that had long plagued Nigerian logistics. With over a decade of experience in freight forwarding and customs clearance, he identified a critical gap — businesses needed a partner they could truly rely on.

Under his leadership, Fastclear has grown from a single Lagos office to a nationwide network spanning 6 cities, completing over 50,000 successful shipments and earning the trust of hundreds of Nigerian businesses.

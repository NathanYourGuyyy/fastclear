FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services, pricing, and customs processes.

With Fastclear's expert customs team, most shipments are cleared within 24–48 hours of arrival at the port. However, the timeline depends on the accuracy of your documentation, cargo type, and NCS workload. We work proactively to submit all documents before your cargo arrives to minimize any delays.

Standard import documents include: Bill of Lading (or Airway Bill), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Form M (for goods above $5,000), and product-specific permits (e.g., NAFDAC for food/drugs, SON for electronics). Our team will guide you through every document requirement for your specific cargo.

Our base rate is ₦8,500 per kilogram for air freight (₦5,000 handling for sea freight), plus a ₦15,000 express surcharge if you choose expedited service. Additional costs include customs duties (varies by HS code), port handling fees, and inland delivery. Use our online calculator for a quick estimate, then contact us for a detailed quote.

For states without a direct hub, we use our trusted haulage partners for last-mile delivery. Transit from Lagos to other hub cities typically takes 24–48 hours.

We take full accountability for every shipment in our care. In the rare case of a delay, you will be notified immediately with an updated timeline. For shipments covered by our cargo insurance, we process claims promptly. Contact our support team as soon as you notice an issue — we have a dedicated resolution team available to investigate and resolve all shipment concerns.
